1. worldserver.conf
Expansion = 2
MaxPlayerLevel = 60

2. playerbots.conf
Set:
AiPlayerbot.RandomBotMinLevel = 1
AiPlayerbot.RandomBotMaxLevel = 60
AiPlayerbot.DeleteRandomBotAccounts = 1
3. Start worldserver

Let it delete the old random-bot accounts.

4. Stop worldserver
Change:
AiPlayerbot.DeleteRandomBotAccounts = 0

5. Start worldserver again
It will generate your new bot population using the 1–60 range.
That's it.
Don't manually delete anything from SQL. Don't touch your real player accounts. Don't change Expansion.

When you beat AQ40, change the two caps:

MaxPlayerLevel = 70
AiPlayerbot.RandomBotMaxLevel = 70

Then unlock Outland.

After Black Temple:

MaxPlayerLevel = 80
AiPlayerbot.RandomBotMaxLevel = 80

Then unlock Northrend.
